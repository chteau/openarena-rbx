# Toned-down player models and skins

Modified copies of OpenArena 0.8.8 art (GPL-2.0, same licence as the
originals), kept at the same paths as in the pk3s. `tools/oaconv` reads this
folder after every pk3, so these files replace the originals in `build/`.

**Only beret's `skin1*` ships.** Every other model below is dropped from
the Roblox roster (`tools/oaconv/roster.json`, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md "Player
roster"), so `oaconv convert` skips these files like the originals. They stay
for the repo's history, and `oaconv tone` still rebuilds them. Beret's
`skin1*` are listed under `toned` in the roster: the converter takes these
copies and never the pk3 originals (a test checks the converted PNGs' sha256).

Why: the female player models were too revealing for Roblox's rules on
suggestive content. They keep their faces, hair, colours and silhouettes; the
chest is now flat to modestly athletic, the buttocks are smaller, nothing
suggests nipples, and the two most exposed outfits cover the chest, midriff
and hips.

Before/after: `docs/toned-models.jpg` (shapes, in game),
`docs/toned-skins.jpg` (the earlier neko/angelyss repaint) and
`docs/toned-beret.jpg` (beret's t-shirt, in ioquake3).

## Generated files

Everything below except the icons and `retroclothes.tga` is generated by
`oaconv tone` from the pk3 originals, the hand-painted bases in
`assets/toned-src/`, and `assets/toned-models.json`:

```sh
cd tools/oaconv && cargo run --release -- tone [--preview DIR]
```

It rewrites the files here; `--preview` also writes clay before/after sheets
per model. A test (`md3edit::tests::overlay_matches_the_config`) fails when
the committed files no longer match what the config builds.

### Shapes (`.md3`)

| Model | Parts (with every LOD shipped) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `sorceress` | `upper`, `lower` | chest and buttocks flattened |
| `kyonshi` | `upper`, `upper_1`, `upper_2` | chest flattened (the robe's `u_torso` only) |
| `major` | `upper`, `lower` | chest and buttocks flattened |
| `neko` | `lower` (her whole body) | chest and buttocks flattened |
| `angelyss` | `lower`, `lower_1`, `lower_2` (her whole body) | chest and buttocks flattened |

Each region in the config is an ellipsoid on a reference frame. A smooth
surface is fitted to the body just above and below it, and the vertices are
pulled in to that surface (keeping 10 % of the old relief, never pushing
outward), with a soft falloff to the region's edge. The same offset follows
each vertex through every animation frame, rotated with its neighbourhood,
so the shape stays put while the model moves; normals are recomputed from
the new surface. LODs take the full model's offsets. Only vertex positions
and normals change: frames, tags, surfaces, UVs and triangles are byte for
byte the originals.

### Hand-made

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `models/players/neko/icon_{default,red,blue}.tga`, `iconh_old.tga` | re-rendered from the new skins; `iconh_old` is now framed on the face |
| `models/players/angelyss/retroclothes.tga` | bikini strings and crotch piece painted to match the suit |
| `models/players/angelyss/icon_retro.tga` | re-rendered from the new skin |

The neko and angelyss suits in `assets/toned-src/` are painted only inside
the UV islands that map to the torso and hips, chosen by each texel's
position on the idle-pose mesh, with soft shading taken from the original
art. Angelyss's red, blue and dark skins use `models/players/hnt/` textures
from `pak6-patch085.pk3` (not only the mature pk3, as noted earlier), so they
get the same suit. Every other player icon is framed on the face and stays.

Beret's shirt needs no hand-painted base: `oaconv tone` paints it from the
pk3 originals and the `shirts` entry alone. His `u_body` surface splits into
UV-connected pieces (front and back torso with the upper arms, forearms,
hands, neck bits); the shirt takes the two torso pieces, picked by a point
on each, below a neckline height on the idle pose (`TORSO_STAND`), so it
ends at the neck and at the seam half way down the upper arm, a short
sleeve. Face, head, forearms, hands, vest (its own `skin2*` surface), gear
and legs stay as they were. Its shading is the original's, each material
(the grey tank top, skin) divided by its own mean, blurred over 8 texels and
kept at 60 % contrast, so no muscle, nipple or tank-top edge shows through.

## Angelyss is not playable from these pk3s

The non-mature pk3s ship her `lower*.md3`, skins and textures but no
`upper.md3` or `head.md3`. Her body is entirely in `lower.md3`; in the
mature pk3 `upper.md3` holds only tags (byte for byte neko's `upper.md3`)
and `head.md3` is a one-unit dummy. Copying neko's `upper.md3`/`head.md3`
would make her playable without any mature geometry; that is not done here.

## Angelyss upper and head

The non-mature 0.8.8 pk3s ship Angelyss without `upper.md3` and `head.md3`
(her whole body is in `lower.md3`), so she can't load. Both files here are
straight copies of Neko's from `pak6-patch088.pk3`: Neko's `upper.md3` is
byte-identical to the one in the mature pk3 (tags only), and her `head.md3`
is a small stand-in inside the head, like the mature one.
